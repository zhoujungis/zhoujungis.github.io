2026 Chinese LLM Showdown: GLM, DeepSeek, and Six Rivals Head to Head

2026-07-05 · 15 min · Stack Frame

Introduction: The “Warring States” Era of Chinese LLMs

In 2026, China’s large models entered an unprecedented boom. If 2025 was the year of playing catch-up, then 2026 is the year of leapfrogging on specific fronts — from GLM-5.2 topping the global coding leaderboard, to DeepSeek V4 Pro closing in on GPT-5.5 at one-fifth the price, to Seedance 2.0 being crowned king of video generation worldwide. But behind all that lurks a critical question: too many models, too hard to choose. Every model claims to be “the strongest”, yet in real use — some are great at code but average at reasoning, some write Chinese like a poet but limp at agentic work, some offer unbeatable value for money but sit on a thin ecosystem. This article uses real data and hands-on experience to map out the 2026 landscape of Chinese LLMs and help you find the best pick for every scenario.

Outlook for the Second Half of 2026

Trend 1: Capabilities Converge, Differentiation Moves to the Application Layer

The gaps between models on core capability are narrowing. The competition ahead is no longer about who is stronger but about who fits into the workflow better — just as Kimi’s Agent Swarm and GLM’s open-source ecosystem each stake out their own ground.

Trend 2: Going Agentic Is Irreversible

From Kimi K2.6’s 300-agent swarm to Qwen3.7-Max’s 35-hour autonomous execution, agent capability is moving from “usable” to “genuinely good”. The decisive battleground for the second half of 2026 is agents.

Trend 3: The Open-Source vs Closed-Source Split

GLM-5.2, DeepSeek V4 Pro, and Qwen3.7 hold firmly to the open-source road, while Doubao and Hunyuan go closed source and dig deep into consumers. Each route has its advantages — open source lowers the barrier, closed source polishes the experience. For developers, this is the best of times.

Trend 4: Video Generation Becomes the New Battleground

Seedance 2.0’s runaway lead proves one thing: multimodal is not a bonus feature, it is the main front. In the second half of 2026, both the number and the quality of video models will climb another step.

Conclusion: My Personal Recommended Stack

As with AI coding tools, models should be used in combination: 
My 2026 Chinese-model strategy:

Write code        → GLM-5.2 (the open-source coding king)
Complex reasoning → DeepSeek V4 Pro (math champion + cheap)
Agent tasks       → Kimi K2.6 (swarm collaboration, in a class of its own)
Chinese writing   → Doubao (the most human feel)

Enterprise deploy → Qwen3.7-Max (open source + agents + cheap)
Budget-sensitive  → MiniMax M2.5 (1/10 the price, 95% of the capability)
There is no all-purpose model, but there is an optimal combination.
